VoiceText Paul 2013 bounded “ship” and “less” suffix handlers
=============================================================

Evidence boundary
-----------------

In `FUN_1000a140` at VA 0x1000a140, the short-input path checks the final four
bytes against DAT_100777e0 (`ship`) and DAT_100774d0 (`less`). The checked-in
`binary/vt_pau.dll` `.data` bytes show the NUL-terminated strings at those
addresses. The compared branch and its order relative to the neighboring
`ance`, `ness`, and `ment` cases are in `stage6-deep-text-functions.txt`.

For a 7- or 8-byte `ship` source, the function removes the four-byte suffix,
calls FUN_1000cb30 on the stem, and, if the result is nonempty, appends
`0x07 0x2d 0x37` followed by NUL. The `less` branch uses the same stem lookup
and success rule, then appends `0x38 0x25 0x35` followed by NUL. The common
success path ORs `0x08` into the caller's row flag. No linguistic or phonetic
meaning is assigned to these marker bytes.

`duration.Engine.NormalizePaul2013ShipSuffixContext` and
`NormalizePaul2013LessSuffixContext` port those direct cases through the
loaded embedded dictionary wrapper. Other lengths, surrounding table-driven
cases, and runtime row-for-row parity remain open.

Static pointers: `stage6-deep-text-functions.txt`, lines 258-474. Direct
string bytes: `objdump -s -j .data binary/vt_pau.dll`, rows 0x100774d0 and
0x100777e0.
