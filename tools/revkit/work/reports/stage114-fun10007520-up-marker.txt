VoiceText Paul 2013 `UP` adjacent-row post-handler mutation
==========================================================

Evidence
--------

The branch is in the Ghidra pseudocode for `FUN_10007520` at VA `0x10007520`
in `stage6-phone-record-consumers.txt`, after its `use` rule. The row and
neighbor strings are compared through `FUN_1001c2c0`; the corresponding
instructions are in `vt_pau-objdump-disassembly.txt`. Treat decompiler output
as pseudocode.

Recovered rule
--------------

When a next counted row exists, the current surface is compared bytewise with
`UP` (DLL VA `0x10077868`). The next counted row's surface at
`model + 0x42a1d + row*0x70` is compared with `a` (DLL VA `0x10077718`) and
`the` (DLL VA `0x1007736c`) using the DLL's mapped string comparator. If either
matches, it writes `08 35 00` at
`model + 0x429cb + row*0x70` and ORs bit 3 into the row-state byte at
`model + 0x429a6 + row*0x70`.

`duration.ApplyPaul2013FUN10007520UpMarker` ports the bounded row checks and
writes. The known counted-row pass runs it after the `use` rule for rows that
reach the supported per-row result branches. Other `FUN_10007520` post-handler
rules and runtime row parity remain open.
