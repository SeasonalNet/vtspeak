VoiceText Paul 2013 supported suffix fallback composition
=========================================================

Scope
-----

`duration.Engine.NormalizePaul2013SupportedSuffixFallbackAfter09DC0` joins
the locally ported portions of the ordered fallback after
`FUN_10009dc0` has already missed. It tries the direct 7-8 byte
`FUN_1000a140` handlers in their recovered check order (`ance`, `ness`,
`ment`, `ship`, `less`), then the 9-31 byte `liness` gate, followed by its
directly recovered long `ed`, `er`, and `ly` branches, then the nine literal
rewrites in `FUN_1000b4d0`. A suffix match
whose stem lookup produces no output is recorded and dispatch continues to
the next handler, matching the caller's zero-return fallback order. A
successful output returns the row flags and bytes from that handler.

Evidence and limits
-------------------

The handler order and calling cascade are represented in the Ghidra
pseudocode in `tools/revkit/work/reports/stage6-deep-text-functions.txt` and
`tools/revkit/work/reports/stage6-phone-dictionary-expanded.txt`. The
individual bounded handlers are supported by Stage 69 through Stage 76
reports, with the `liness` gate and `ly` branch added in Stages 89 and 90.
Unsupported short-table indices are reported on the result instead of being
treated as ordinary misses. This composition only wires those already ported
cases. It does not implement `FUN_10009dc0`, the
remaining `FUN_1000a140` branches, or native runtime row parity. Callers must
preserve the earlier `FUN_10009dc0` step and must not interpret an unmatched
result as proof that all native handlers missed.
