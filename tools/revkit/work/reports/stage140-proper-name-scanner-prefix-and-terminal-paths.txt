VoiceText Paul 2013 proper-name scanner prefix and terminal paths
=================================================================

Evidence
--------

The complete Ghidra pseudocode for `FUN_1005a350` is preserved in
`tools/revkit/work/reports/stage135-fun1005a350-pseudo.txt`. At entry, the
scanner consumes leading space, tab, CR, and LF bytes, incrementing output
field `param_1[0]` for each byte and counting LF separately. One LF sets
`param_1[1]` to one. More than one LF returns with status 8 and an empty
scanner string. If the first non-whitespace byte is NUL, the scanner returns
status 9 with an empty scanner string. Both early returns set the scanner
string from `DAT_1009f948`, whose byte is zero in the read-only DLL.

Port
----

`text.ObservePaul2013ProperNameScannerInput` derives the leading-prefix
count, single-LF marker, and terminal status 8/9 result from a NUL-terminated
input. The duration-engine `WithScannerInputAndContextTables` methods combine
that observation with a caller-supplied ordinary scanner output/status,
context-table snapshots, and the already-ported post-scanner gate.

Evidence boundary
-----------------

This ports only the scanner prelude and its multiline/empty early returns.
The input-to-token transformation for ordinary nonempty text remains
unimplemented. The ordinary output and status are explicit inputs, and no
whole-scanner parity is claimed.
