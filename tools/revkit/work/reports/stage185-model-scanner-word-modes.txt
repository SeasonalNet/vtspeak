Stage 185: additional ASCII word modes and common dispatch correction

Evidence

- FUN_1005a350 original x86 at 1005ac34-1005acb9 selects mode 8 first,
  excludes 9/0xc/0x1a/0x12/0x18/0x1d/0x1c from the common dot/int branch,
  and otherwise tries dot/letter or mapped int followed by apostrophe/letter.
  Mode 0x17 is not excluded: word.next joins before 1005af62 reaches its
  suffix branch. Stage 182's contrary fixture expectation was incorrect and
  is now replaced with the observed native dispatch expectation.
- Modes 0x11/0x15 at 1005b04d-1005b136 additionally join hyphen/letter;
  a single uppercase initial, digit and uppercase following letter; or
  mapped United followed by one literal space and a mapped States prefix
  whose next byte is not a letter. FUN_10062370(remaining,6) at 10062370
  verifies six nonzero bytes, with signed count and signed-short result.
  The space/digit is appended before resuming the common letter loop.
- Mode 0x19 at 1005b136-1005b178 appends a digit after one uppercase letter,
  without a following-letter condition. Later digits do not meet that gate.
- Mode 0x16 at 1005b178-1005b1b7 joins apostrophe/letter or hyphen/letter;
  modes 0xc/0x1a at 1005b1b7-1005b1d7 join apostrophe/letter without suffix
  recognition. All such components retain original ASCII spelling/case.
- Modes 9/0x12/0x18/0x1c have no additional ASCII word join in this dispatch.
  Read-only PE tests confirm United at 1009c418 and States at 1009c410,
  alongside the previously recovered int/wi strings.

Implementation and validation

- A common word-mode helper consumes only recovered separator bytes before
  the existing letter loop resumes. Accumulated cost, the >28 pre-copy
  length gate, raw result coordinates and model post-token lookup remain
  shared across modes. Other numeric/nonletter starts explicitly delegate.
- Tests cover every added mode, repeated unrestricted apostrophes, combined
  joins, uppercase/digit gates and their case/length exclusions, mapped
  United States and invalid plural/short/whitespace variants, truncation,
  cost overflow, rollback, post-token flags and the corrected mode-0x17
  common dot/int dispatch. Proprietary DLL literals are checked read-only.
- Changed Go files formatted. From engine/ with GOCACHE=/tmp/vtspeak-go-cache,

Limits
