VoiceText Paul 2013 FUN_1000a140 short `ly` suffix
=================================================

Evidence
--------

The `FUN_1000a140` fallback at
`tools/revkit/work/reports/stage6-dictionary-record-functions.txt`, around
lines 1379-1445, rejects source lengths below five and compares the mapped
final two bytes with `DAT_10077784` (`ly`). It removes `ly` and calls
`FUN_1000cb30`. On a miss, it reads the short-table value at `length-4`; when
that value is zero and the mapped byte at `length-3` is `i`, it retries with
that byte replaced by `y`. A nonempty result changes a trailing `&` to `0x07`,
adds `+` unless the output already ends in `+`, then appends `&` and sets
row-flag bit 3.

Implementation
--------------

`NormalizePaul2013LySuffixContext` applies this same bounded branch for
5- to 31-byte inputs. The existing `NormalizePaul2013LongLySuffixContext`
entry point delegates to it for compatibility. The supported suffix cascade
invokes the handler after the short `ers`, `est`, `ing`, `ist`, and `ful`
handlers and before its terminal-`s` handler, matching the direct suffix
order.
