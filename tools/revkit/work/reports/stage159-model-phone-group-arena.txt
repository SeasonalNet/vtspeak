Limits

- Group decoding uses the established 0x01..0x45 codebook. It rejects M and
  other structural symbols; their direct DAT_1007b6c0/DAT_1007baa8 mappings
  are not recovered by this adapter. This is a supported-input arena port,
  not whole-arena native runtime parity or complete synthesis parity.

  remain separate work. Returned descriptors are existing Go value views.
