Stage 151: bounded VTML substitution expansion

Runtime evidence

- The Stage 16 report records `<vtml_sub alias="Hello world.">x</vtml_sub>`
  producing a WAVE byte-identical to the plain `Hello world.` input under text
  formats 0, 4, 6, and 8. The `x` control produced a different WAVE.
- This establishes alias expansion for that captured ASCII fixture only. It
  does not establish general VTML parsing, attribute order, escaping, nested
  markup, malformed-input behavior, or source-coordinate mapping.

Ported behavior

- `text.ExpandPaul2013CapturedVTMLSubstitutions` replaces the body of the
  exact `<vtml_sub alias="...">surface</vtml_sub>` shape with its printable
  ASCII alias and preserves surrounding plain text.
- The helper rejects other markup, extra attributes, nested body markup,
  empty aliases, and aliases with non-ASCII or XML-escaped characters.
- Tests cover the captured form, surrounding text, multiple tags, and the
  fail-closed cases. Expansion returns text only; callers that need source
  spans must supply their own mapping.

Limits

- No new DLL differential was run. No claim is made for attribute escapes,
  alternative attribute order/case, nested substitutions, or audio output
  beyond the Stage 16 fixture.
