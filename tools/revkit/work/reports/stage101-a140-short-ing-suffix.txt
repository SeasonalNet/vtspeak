VoiceText Paul 2013 FUN_1000a140 short `ing` suffix
==================================================

Evidence
--------

The `FUN_1000a140` pseudocode in
`tools/revkit/work/reports/stage6-dictionary-record-functions.txt`, around
lines 973-1157, compares the mapped final three bytes with `DAT_10077378`
(`ing`). The short-table predicate reads offsets `length-5` and `length-4`.
In the branch that may inspect `length-7` and `length-6`, the decompiler
pseudocode first checks `length < 7`; that condition skips both reads for
short inputs. The selected stem is then looked up through `FUN_1000cbe0`
with `%`, followed by the generic `FUN_1000cb30` fallback. A nonempty result
gets the `#.` tail and row-flag bit 3 through the common success path.

Implementation
--------------

`NormalizePaul2013IngSuffixContext` applies this branch for 5- to 31-byte
ASCII C strings. It retains the minimum `length-5` and `length-4` table
indexes, avoids the guarded earlier-pair reads below seven bytes, preserves
the selected stem order, and appends `#.` on success. The previous
`NormalizePaul2013LongIngSuffixContext` method delegates to the generalized
entry point. The short cascade tries this branch after `ers` and `est`,
before `ist`, `ful`, `ly`, and terminal `s`.
