Stage 145: narrow VTML pause rule adapter

Ported behavior

- `text.Paul2013InlinePauseModelSourceRule` selects the observed
  `<vtml_pause` table entry at handler address `0x1002fb10` and binds the
  supported exact `<vtml_pause time="N"/>` lexical form to the existing
  decimal duration parser.
- The dispatcher adapter consumes the tag, writes the synthetic separating
  space used by the inline-pause text path, and reports the source byte offset,
  duration in milliseconds, and 16 kHz frame count to a caller callback.
  Token-boundary placement stays with the caller because this rule does not
  tokenize surrounding text.
- `LexiconFrontend.ResolveTextWithPaul2013InlinePauses` now sends each markup
  boundary through the recovered longest-key dispatcher, so its supported
  pause form uses the same handler and decimal parser as direct dispatcher
  callers.
- Unsupported attribute names, malformed or non-decimal values, and other
  tag forms decline the selected rule. The generic dispatcher restores its
  cursors on decline.

Evidence and limits

- `tools/revkit/work/reports/vt_pau-objdump-disassembly.txt` at `0x1002fb10`
  shows a wrapper that forwards its arguments to `FUN_1002f8e0` with selector
  6. The adapter connects the separately captured self-closing lexical form;
  it does not port the shared routine's full settings, marker, token-index, or
  caller-state behavior.
- The observed rule-table key and handler address are listed in
  `stage144-model-source-rule-table.txt`. The exact supported lexical form and
  frame conversion are covered by `engine/text/vtml_pause_test.go`.
- No original-DLL differential was run for this adapter. Full VTML pause
  behavior, `<vt_pause>`, alternative attribute layouts, and pipeline timing
  remain open.
