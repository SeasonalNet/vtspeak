VoiceText Paul 2013 FUN_1000a140 terminal-s suffix
==================================================

Evidence
--------

The Ghidra pseudocode for `FUN_1000a140` handles five- to 31-byte C strings at
`tools/revkit/work/reports/stage6-dictionary-record-functions.txt`, around
lines 1447-1610. It first checks the mapped final byte for `s`. The branch
uses `FUN_1001c320`, whose pseudocode at
`tools/revkit/work/reports/stage6-char-and-table-functions.txt` compares a
bounded number of character-map values. The two-byte literals at DLL VAs
`0x100778d8`, `0x100778dc`, and `0x100778e0` are `ie`, `sh`, and `ch`; the
NUL-terminated `szx` bytes begin at `0x100778e4`. The short-table values used
by this branch are the `a`-to-`z` entries at `DAT_10077d5e`, cataloged in
Stage 75. `od` inspection of `binary/vt_pau.dll` confirms the literal order in
the `.data` region.

Recovered branch order
----------------------

1. When the byte before final `s` maps to `e` and the preceding mapped bytes
   meet the `szx`, `ch`, `sh`, or table-gated `o` condition, normalize the
   source without final `s`; on a miss, retry without `es`.
2. Otherwise, when the short-table value at source offset `length-4` is zero
   and the mapped two-byte sequence at `length-3` is `ie`, replace that `i`
   with `y` and normalize the resulting stem.
3. Otherwise, inspect the mapped pair ending immediately before final `s`
   and the short-table value at `length-3`. The ordinary reduction removes
   `s`, retrying without `es` when the preceding byte maps to `e`. The other
   reductions remove `es`; the native protected-output branch rejects empty
   output and results ending in `0x14`, `)`, `7`, `8`, `D`, or `E`.
4. On success, append `7` after outputs ending in space, `*`, `5`, `9`, or `:`;
   append `#D` after outputs ending in `0x14`, `)`, `7`, `8`, `D`, or `E`;
   otherwise append `D`. The common tail sets row-flag bit 3.

Implementation
--------------

`NormalizePaul2013SSuffixContext` ports these ordered branches and uses
the generic `FUN_1000cb30` adapter for stem lookup. Its fixed-width comparisons
map exactly two bytes, matching `FUN_1001c320`. Stage 106 later captures the
full signed-index short-table window; the compatibility
`TableClassUnsupported` field is no longer set for these reads. The handler runs after the
short `ers`, `est`, `ing`, `ist`, `ful`, and `ly` cases and the long `liness`,
`ing`, `ist`, `ful`, and `ly` cases. For long inputs, it leaves the separate
`liness` branch to its earlier handler. The old terminal-`s` gate no longer
rejects these inputs before the port runs.

Evidence boundary
-----------------

This is a static pseudocode port. No original-runtime row-for-row comparison
has been made. The table window and adjacent `.data` values are statically
recovered; exception-resource/state production, other A140 branches, and
full C3A0 runtime parity remain open. The historical Stage 91 gate is retained
as a record of the earlier fail-closed boundary; its current status is noted
there.
